1 · Scope: the group exceeds the converted threshold

For a third-country parent, the €750M threshold converts to local currency at the FX rate of 21 December 2021, rounded to the nearest thousand Art. 48c(9), 2nd para. It must be exceeded in each of the last two consecutive financial years DA 11ª ap. primero.4.

2 · Deadlines: Spain halves the Directive's timeline

Approval, publication and Registro Mercantil filing within 6 months of year end DA 11ª ap. tercero.1 — the Directive's outer bound is 12 months Art. 48d(1). First financial year in scope: starting on or after 22 June 2024 DA 11ª ap. quinto.

3 · Obligations of the Spanish subsidiary

!

Publish the ultimate parent's consolidated public CbCR report — now overdue for FY2025

The subsidiary publishes the US parent's group-level report, not a Spain-only extract. Unless it qualifies as a small entity (2 of: assets ≤ €4M, turnover ≤ €8M, ≤ 50 employees — implausible here) it is in scope. DA 11ª ap. primero.4 Ley 22/2015 art. 3.9

!

File with the Registro Mercantil together with the annual accounts

A Spain-specific obligation on top of the Directive. DA 11ª ap. tercero.1

!

Keep the report freely accessible on a website for at least 5 years, in an official EU language

The subsidiary's own website suffices. DA 11ª ap. tercero.2(b), tercero.3

?

If the parent withholds data: request it formally, then publish what is held plus a non-availability statement

Document the written request — it is the subsidiary's defence. DA 11ª ap. primero.4, 2nd para

✓

Escape route: the US parent may publish an equivalent report itself

Free, machine-readable, on its website, in an official EU language, naming one EU subsidiary/branch as filer — and within 6 months under the Spanish text DA 11ª ap. primero.6, where the Directive allows 12 Art. 48b(6)(a)(iii). A parent publishing in month 10 satisfies the Directive but not the Spanish provision.
